# Pitch Outline (5 slides, about 3 minutes)

Mapped to the pitch judging criteria.

## Slide 1: The problem (20%)
- Practice questions tell you "wrong" but not **why**.
- Share your own AZ-900 moment: a question you missed and didn't understand.
- Result: learners memorise answers instead of understanding Azure.

## Slide 2: The solution (25%)
- **AZ-900 Smart Quiz Builder**: instant feedback plus an explanation on every answer.
- Organised by the 3 skill areas.
- **Live demo:** pick a topic, answer one wrong on purpose, show the explanation, finish, then show "Retry missed."

## Slide 3: Who it's for (15%)
- First-time AZ-900 candidates, study groups and cohorts, career changers.
- Use case: 10-minute focused practice on one weak topic.

## Slide 4: Impact and scalability (20%)
- Free, no login, works on any phone.
- Swap the question set to cover AI-900, AZ-104 and other certifications.
- Next: AI "explain differently" button, timed exam mode, leaderboards with Supabase.

## Slide 5: How it was built (clarity and delivery, 20%)
- Claude.ai for prompt design, question drafting and the build.
- Structured prompts plus a review pass for accuracy (see `PROMPTS.md`).
- Repo, README and live demo link.
- Close: "Learners became builders, and the next cohort gets the tool they needed."

## Demo checklist
- [ ] Open the live link on your phone before presenting
- [ ] Rehearse the missed-question demo path
- [ ] Mention that the tool is unofficial and all questions are original
